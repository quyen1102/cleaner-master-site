Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

Summary

Spacey never uploads your photos, videos, or library information. Every scan and cleanup action runs locally on your iPhone or iPad. The only information Spacey can receive is the feedback you choose to send from Settings (see “Feedback You Send”).

On-Device Analysis

Photo and video analysis — including duplicate detection, similarity grouping, blur and shake detection, screenshot and document detection, and HEIC/HEVC compression — runs entirely on your device. Your photos and videos are never uploaded to any server.

Feedback You Send

In Settings → Rate Spacey, if you tell us you are not satisfied, you can send feedback. Sending is always your choice and happens only when you tap Send. The feedback includes:

  • the topics you select and the text you type;
  • an email address, only if you choose to enter one so we can reply;
  • technical details: app version, iOS version, device model identifier, and language.

It never includes photos, videos, file names, or any information about your photo library. We use it only to understand problems, improve Spacey, and reply to you. It is not used for advertising or tracking and is not sold or shared with third parties for their own purposes. Feedback is stored in a spreadsheet on Google Workspace (Google Sheets) operated for us, which processes it on our behalf. We keep feedback only as long as needed to improve the app. To access or delete feedback you sent, contact us through the Support page and include the email you entered.

If you prefer, you can send feedback by email instead; in that case your email provider handles the message under its own policy.

No Accounts, Analytics, Ads, or Tracking

  • Spacey has no account or sign-in system.
  • The app contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs.
  • This website also uses no cookies, no analytics, and no trackers.

Local Storage & Uninstall

Scan results and app preferences are stored locally on your device. All locally stored data is permanently removed when you delete Spacey from your device.

iCloud Photos

If your photo library uses iCloud Photos, items not currently stored locally may be downloaded by Apple's Photos framework (PhotoKit) when needed for analysis. Downloads are set to Wi-Fi only by default in the app and are handled directly by iOS under Apple's privacy policy.

User-Initiated Deletion & Compression

Deleting or compressing photos and videos only happens when you choose to start it. Removal always goes through the standard iOS confirmation dialog, and deleted items move to the system Recently Deleted album where you can restore them for 30 days. When compressing media, originals are only removed after the new compressed copy is verified and you confirm.

Children's Privacy

Spacey is not directed to children under 13. Apart from optional feedback typed by the user, Spacey does not collect personal information, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy in the future, we will publish the updated version on this page and revise the effective date above.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how Spacey handles your photo library, please reach out through our Support page.
