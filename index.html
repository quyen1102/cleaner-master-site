Spacey

Photo Cleaner

Coming soon to the App Store

Find duplicates, near-identical shots, blurry photos, old screenshots, receipts, and large videos in your camera roll — and reclaim storage in a few taps without your photos ever leaving your phone.

What Spacey Does

  • Exact duplicates & similar shots Groups identical copies (keeping one automatically) and picks the best shot from bursts and look-alikes based on sharpness, exposure, and faces.
  • Blurry & shaky photos Detects out-of-focus and motion-blurred pictures you will never use so you can clear them out fast.
  • Screenshots, documents & large videos Surfaces old screenshots, photos of paper, receipts, slides, and whiteboards, plus your biggest videos sorted by size.
  • Compress instead of delete Convert photos to HEIC and videos to HEVC to save space with little visible quality loss. Originals are only removed after verification and your confirmation.
  • Safe by design Review everything before removal. Deletions always use the standard iOS confirmation and move items to Recently Deleted for 30 days.
  • Fast, resumable & private Adaptive on-device scanning with background continuation, Live Activities, Home & Lock Screen widgets, Full or Limited access, and Wi-Fi-only iCloud Photos downloads by default.
