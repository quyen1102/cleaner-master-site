Support & FAQ

Answers to common questions about Cleaner Master for iPhone and iPad.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does scanning work?

Cleaner Master scans your photo library entirely on your device to group exact duplicates, find similar and burst shots (picking the best shot based on sharpness, exposure, and faces), detect blurry or shaky photos, surface old screenshots and documents/receipts, and list large videos by size. Results appear as the scan runs so you can start reviewing right away.

What is the difference between Full and Limited photo access, and how do I change it?

Cleaner Master works with both Full Access and Limited Access. With Limited Access, only the photos you explicitly select are visible to the app. To change your choice anytime, open iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Cleaner Master (or Settings → Apps → Cleaner Master → Photos) and choose Full Access or update your Selected Photos.

How do I recover deleted photos?

Every deletion uses the standard iOS confirmation dialog and moves items to the Recently Deleted album in Apple Photos rather than erasing them immediately. You can restore deleted photos and videos from Recently Deleted for up to 30 days.

Is photo and video compression safe?

Yes. Compression converts photos to HEIC and videos to HEVC to save storage with little visible quality loss. Cleaner Master verifies the new compressed copy first, and your original file is only removed after you confirm.

Why does a scan pause sometimes?

Cleaner Master uses smart, adaptive scanning that respects your device's temperature, battery level, Low Power Mode, and memory. If your device gets warm or battery runs low, the scan automatically slows down or pauses, then picks up right where it left off once conditions improve.

How does Cleaner Master handle iCloud Photos and cellular data?

Cleaner Master supports iCloud Photos. When a full photo or video is stored in iCloud and needed for analysis, Apple's Photos framework downloads it on-device. Downloads are set to Wi-Fi only by default so scanning does not unexpectedly consume your cellular data.

How do background scans and Live Activities work?

Scans you start can continue in the background and resume from where they stopped. You can follow scan progress directly from your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island using Live Activities, and pause or stop a scan anytime.

What do the widgets show?

Cleaner Master provides Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets so you can see at a glance how much storage space you can free up without opening the app.

Does Cleaner Master collect any of my data?

No. 100% of scanning and compression runs locally on your device. There are no accounts, no uploads, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking SDKs. Read the full Privacy Policy for details.

Need Help or Found a Bug?

If your question is not answered above or you would like to report an issue, please open a ticket on our GitHub Issues page. Include your iOS/iPadOS version and steps to reproduce the issue if applicable.
